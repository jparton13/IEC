What We Do

Manufacturing performance is engineered.

The factory is a system. Products, people, equipment, material, information, facilities, and capital all interact to determine performance.

IEC brings industrial engineering and operations leadership together to design that system intentionally — from today's constraints to tomorrow's requirements.
